Dr. Cristina Schaurich is a trained pediatric neurosurgeon who completed her training in Pediatric Neurosurgery and Epilepsy Surgery at BC Children’s Hospital in 2022. She earned an MSc in Surgery from the University of British Columbia in 2023, where her research examined dynamic skull development during the first eight years of life. Dr. Schaurich is currently a PhD candidate in the Experimental Medicine Program at UBC and a researcher in the Brain Behaviour Lab. Her work focuses on developmental neuroanatomy, investigating structural brain changes during early childhood, with particular emphasis on the maturation of cortico-cerebellar pathways.  Dr. Schaurich is also a Neuroanatomy lecturer at UBC, exploring the use of emerging technologies, including virtual and augmented reality, to advance pediatric neuroanatomy education.
